# Shared Drives

This fictional standard describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Team drives

Each team has a shared drive. Files that belong to the team's work are stored there, not in personal drives.

## Sharing outside the company

Files on shared drives can be shared outside the company only with named people, never with a public link.

## When someone leaves

Files in a leaver's personal drive are moved to their manager after 30 days and deleted after 90 days.
