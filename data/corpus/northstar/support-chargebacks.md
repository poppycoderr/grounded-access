# Handling Chargebacks

This fictional procedure is for the Northstar Cloud support department only. A chargeback happens when a customer disputes a card payment with their bank instead of asking Northstar Cloud for a refund.

## When a chargeback arrives

The payment provider notifies the billing team, which opens a support ticket with the dispute reason and the deadline for a response. The agent assigned to the ticket contacts the customer within one business day to find out whether the dispute was intended.

## Responding to the bank

If the customer agrees that the charge was correct, the agent asks them to withdraw the dispute with their bank. Otherwise the agent collects the invoice, the usage records for the billed period and any correspondence with the customer, and the billing team submits them to the payment provider at least three days before the deadline.

## The account during a dispute

The account stays active during a dispute. If a customer has two open chargebacks at the same time, the agent asks a team lead whether to require a different payment method before the next invoice.
