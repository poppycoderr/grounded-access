# Loaner Laptops

This fictional guide describes how Northstar Cloud works.

## When

Employees whose laptop is being repaired receive a loaner laptop from the IT service desk on the same day.

## Data

Loaner laptops are wiped when they are returned. Files are kept in shared drives, never only on the loaner.

## Returning

Loaner laptops are returned within two working days after the employee's own laptop is back.
