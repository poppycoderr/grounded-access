# Pricing Guide

This fictional guide lists Northstar Cloud list prices and discount limits. From this version on it is for the sales and support departments only.

## List prices

The Standard plan costs 450 EUR per month. The Enterprise plan costs 2,100 EUR per month.

## Discounts

Annual prepayment earns a 10 percent discount on either plan. An account executive may offer up to 15 percent without approval; anything above that needs the sales director.

## Price changes

Existing customers keep their old price until their next renewal.
