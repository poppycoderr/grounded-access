# Shift Handover

This fictional guide is for the Northstar Cloud support department only.

## Handover notes

At the end of each shift the outgoing agents write a handover note listing open P1 and P2 tickets and promised callbacks.

## Overlap

Shifts overlap by 30 minutes so that the handover can be discussed in person or on a call.

## Follow-the-sun

Open P1 tickets are handed from the EU team to the US team at 17:00 Central European Time, never left without an owner.
