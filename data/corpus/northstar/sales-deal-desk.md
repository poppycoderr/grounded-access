# Deal Desk Approvals

This fictional procedure is confidential and is for the sales department only. It explains which non-standard contract terms need approval from the deal desk.

## What needs the deal desk

Any contract with payment terms longer than 60 days, a liability cap above the annual contract value, a termination right without cause, or a commitment to host data in a region the product does not yet offer goes to the deal desk before it is sent to the customer.

## How to submit

The account executive submits the draft contract and a one-page summary of the deviation in the deal desk queue. The summary states why the customer needs the term and what the account is worth over three years.

## Response times

The deal desk answers within two business days. During the last ten days of a quarter it answers within one business day, and requests received after 15:00 on the last day of a quarter are handled in the next quarter.

## Escalation

If the deal desk declines a term, the account executive may escalate to the chief revenue officer, whose decision is final. Each escalation is recorded in the deal desk queue.
