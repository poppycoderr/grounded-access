# Refunds and Credits

This fictional guide tells support agents when Northstar Cloud customers can get money back.

## Refunds

Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment. Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within 30 days of renewal.

## Account credits

Instead of a refund, agents may offer account credit worth up to 20 percent of the monthly fee after a verified billing mistake.

## Approval

Refunds above 1,000 USD need approval from the finance team before the agent confirms them to the customer.
