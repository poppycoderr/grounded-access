# Access Review and Offboarding

This fictional standard describes how Northstar Cloud keeps access to systems up to date.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every employee account. Hardware security keys are required for anyone with production access.

## Quarterly reviews

Managers review the access of their direct reports every quarter and remove any entitlement that is no longer needed for the current role.

## Offboarding

All access of a departing employee is revoked within 24 hours of their last working day. Shared credentials the person knew are rotated within 5 working days.
