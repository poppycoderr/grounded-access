# Third-party Licences

This fictional standard is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## Allowed licences

Libraries under permissive licences such as MIT, BSD and Apache 2.0 can be used without approval.

## Licences that need approval

Libraries under copyleft licences need approval from the legal team before they are added to a product that customers receive.

## Checking

The build fails when a library under a licence that is not on the allowed list is added.
