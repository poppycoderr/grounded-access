# Employment References

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Who gives references

Only People Operations confirms employment to outside parties. It confirms the start date, the end date and the last job title, and nothing else unless the employee asks in writing.

## Personal references

Managers may give a personal reference for a former employee, but they must say clearly that it is personal and not on behalf of the company.

## Requests by phone

Reference requests by phone are not answered. The caller is asked to send the request by email.
