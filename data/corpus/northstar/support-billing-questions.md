# Answering Billing Questions

This fictional guide is for the Northstar Cloud support department only.

## Invoices

Agents can resend invoices and explain line items, but cannot change an invoice that has been finalized.

## Corrections

Corrections to finalized invoices are made by the billing team as credit notes.

## Payment methods

Agents never take card numbers by email, chat or phone; customers change payment methods in the console.
