# Mentoring Program

This fictional program pairs Northstar Cloud employees with a mentor from another team.

## Joining

Any employee who has completed their probation period can ask for a mentor. Mentors volunteer and must have been with the company for at least two years.

## Meetings

A mentor and mentee meet at least once a month for six months. The time counts as working time and does not need to be booked as leave or learning days.

## Matching

The people team matches pairs twice a year, in February and in August. A mentor is never matched with someone in their own reporting line.
