# Customer Satisfaction Survey

This fictional guide is for the Northstar Cloud support department only.

## When it is sent

A short survey is sent to the customer 24 hours after a ticket is solved, at most once per customer per month.

## Low scores

A team lead reads every survey with a score of one or two out of five within two business days and contacts the customer if the comment asks for it.

## Targets

The team aims for an average score of at least 4.3 per quarter.
