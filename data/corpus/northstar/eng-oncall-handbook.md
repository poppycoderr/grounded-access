# On-call Handbook

This fictional handbook explains how on-call works for Northstar Cloud engineering teams.

## Rotations

Each team runs a weekly rotation that starts on Monday at 10:00 local time. Handover happens in a 15-minute call between the outgoing and incoming engineer.

## Acknowledging pages

The on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 5 minutes. If a page is not acknowledged within 15 minutes, it escalates automatically to the secondary on-call engineer.

## Severity levels

A SEV1 is a full outage of a customer-facing service or any confirmed data loss. A SEV2 is a partial degradation that affects more than 5 percent of requests. Everything else is a SEV3.

## Compensation

Engineers receive an on-call allowance of 250 EUR per week of primary on-call, plus time off in lieu for any page answered between 22:00 and 07:00.
