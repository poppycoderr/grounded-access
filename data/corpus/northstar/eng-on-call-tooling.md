# On-call Tooling

This fictional guide is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## Paging app

On-call engineers install the paging app on their company phone and test it at the start of each rotation.

## Laptop

The on-call engineer keeps their laptop within reach and can open the incident channel within five minutes.

## Handover notes

Every rotation ends with written handover notes in the team's on-call log.
