# Travel Safety

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Before travelling

Business trips are booked through the travel tool so that the security team knows where employees are.

## Risky destinations

Trips to countries on the security team's watch list need approval from the head of security two weeks before travel.

## Emergencies abroad

Employees in trouble abroad call the 24-hour assistance number on the back of their badge.
