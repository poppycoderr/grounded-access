# Office Attendance

This fictional policy describes how Northstar Cloud works.

## Expectation

Teams meet in the office at least two days per week, on days the team agrees together.

## Exceptions

Employees who are remote-eligible under the remote work policy may agree a different pattern with their manager.

## Travel

Travel to the office is not reimbursed for employees who live more than 100 km away; they come at least once a quarter, and that trip is reimbursed.
