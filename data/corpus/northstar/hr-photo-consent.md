# Photos and Recordings at Work

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Consent

Photos and videos of employees are only published, internally or externally, with their consent. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

## Company events

At company events a photographer wears a visible badge, and employees who do not want to be photographed receive a coloured lanyard.

## Meeting recordings

Meetings are only recorded if every participant is told at the start. Recordings are deleted after 90 days.
