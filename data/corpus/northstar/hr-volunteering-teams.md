# Team Volunteering Days

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Organizing a team day

A team that wants to volunteer together chooses a charity from the approved list and books a date with People Operations.

## Transport

Transport to the volunteering site is booked by the team and paid by the company.

## Approved list

Charities are added to the approved list after a check by the legal team, which takes about two weeks.
