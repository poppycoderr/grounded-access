# Disaster Recovery Plan

This fictional plan describes how Northstar Cloud restores its services after the loss of a whole region.

## Recovery tiers

Services are grouped into three tiers. Tier 1 services are restored first, within one hour. Tier 2 services are restored within four hours, and tier 3 services within two days.

## Declaring a disaster

The incident commander declares a disaster when a region has been unavailable for 30 minutes and the cloud provider gives no recovery estimate.

## Exercises

The plan is exercised once a year by restoring every tier 1 service in the standby region.
