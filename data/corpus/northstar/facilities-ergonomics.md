# Ergonomic Assessments

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Office desks

All desks in the offices can be raised and lowered. A guide to setting up a desk is on each monitor arm.

## Assessments

Employees with back, neck or wrist pain can ask for an ergonomic assessment, which takes place within two weeks.

## Special equipment

Equipment recommended by the assessment, such as a special mouse or chair, is ordered by the facilities team.
