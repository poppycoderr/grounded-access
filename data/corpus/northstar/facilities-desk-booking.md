# Desk Booking

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Booking a desk

Desks in all offices are booked in the workplace app, up to two weeks in advance. A booking that is not checked in by 10:30 is released.

## Team areas

Teams can reserve a team area for one fixed day per week. On other days its desks are open to everyone.

## Leaving a desk

Desks are cleared at the end of each day. Personal items are kept in a locker.
