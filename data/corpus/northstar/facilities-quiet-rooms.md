# Quiet Rooms

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Purpose

Quiet rooms are for rest, prayer, meditation or breastfeeding. They cannot be booked for meetings or calls.

## Use

Rooms are used for at most 30 minutes at a time. The sign on the door shows whether the room is free.

## Breastfeeding

Each office has a fridge reserved for breast milk in the quiet room.
