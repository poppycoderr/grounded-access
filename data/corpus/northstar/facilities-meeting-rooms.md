# Meeting Rooms

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Booking

Meeting rooms are booked in the calendar. A room that nobody enters within ten minutes of the start is released automatically.

## Large rooms

Rooms for more than twelve people can be booked for at most two hours, unless the facilities team approves a longer booking.

## Video calls

Every room has a video call system. Problems are reported with the QR code on the table.
