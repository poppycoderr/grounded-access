# Clean Desk and Clear Screen

This fictional standard describes how Northstar Cloud works.

## Clean desk

Papers with confidential information are locked away when a desk is left for more than a few minutes and at the end of the day.

## Clear screen

Screens lock automatically after five minutes without input. Employees lock their screen whenever they leave the desk.

## Whiteboards

Whiteboards in meeting rooms are wiped at the end of every meeting.
