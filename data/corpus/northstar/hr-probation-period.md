# Probation Period

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Length

New employees start with a probation period of six months. The period can be extended once, by up to three months, if both the employee and the manager agree in writing.

## Review meetings

The manager holds a review meeting after three months and another two weeks before the probation period ends. Both meetings are written up and shared with the employee.

## Notice during probation

During probation either side can end the employment with two weeks' notice. After probation the notice periods of the employment contract apply.
