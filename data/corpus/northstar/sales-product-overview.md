# Northstar Cloud Product Overview

Northstar Cloud offers a fictional subscription billing platform for software companies.

## Plans

The Standard plan targets companies with up to 10,000 subscribers. The Enterprise plan adds single sign-on, audit logs and a 99.95 percent uptime commitment.

## Data residency

Customers can choose to store their data in the EU region (Frankfurt) or the US region (Oregon). Data does not move between regions unless the customer requests a migration.
