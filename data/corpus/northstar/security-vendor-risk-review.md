# Vendor Risk Review

This fictional standard is confidential. It describes how Northstar Cloud assesses the suppliers that process its data or customer data.

## Vendor tiers

A vendor that stores or processes customer data is tier 1. A vendor that processes only employee data or internal data is tier 2. A vendor that holds no company data at all is tier 3.

## Review frequency

Tier 1 vendors are reviewed every 12 months and before every contract renewal. Tier 2 vendors are reviewed every 24 months. Tier 3 vendors are reviewed once, before the first contract.

## What a review covers

A review checks the vendor's most recent independent audit report, where the vendor stores data, which subprocessors it uses and how quickly it must report a breach to Northstar Cloud. A tier 1 vendor must report a breach within 24 hours.

## Findings

A tier 1 vendor with an open critical finding may not receive new customer data until the finding is closed. The security team keeps the list of vendors with open findings, and only the chief information security officer can accept a critical finding as a risk.
