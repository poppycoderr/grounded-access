# Relocation Support

This fictional policy covers employees who move to a new city at Northstar Cloud's request.

## What is covered

Northstar Cloud pays for economy flights for the employee and their immediate family, shipping of up to 20 cubic meters of household goods, and temporary housing for up to 30 nights.

## Relocation allowance

A lump-sum relocation allowance of one month's base salary is paid with the first salary in the new location.

## Repayment

Employees who resign within 12 months of the move repay the relocation costs pro rata.
