# Part-time Work

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Asking for part-time

Employees may ask to reduce their hours after six months of employment. The request is answered within one month.

## Minimum hours

Part-time roles have at least 20 hours per week, except during parental leave arrangements.

## Going back to full time

An employee who reduced their hours may ask to return to full time; the request is granted at the latest after twelve months if a full-time position exists in the team.
