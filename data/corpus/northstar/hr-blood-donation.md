# Blood and Plasma Donation

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Time off

Employees may donate blood or plasma during working hours up to four times a year. The time for the donation and for travel to the donation centre counts as working time.

## Company drives

The People team organizes a blood donation drive in each large office twice a year.

## Rest after donating

Employees who feel unwell after donating may go home for the rest of the day without taking leave.
