# Recognition Program

This fictional program describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Peer awards

Any employee can give a colleague a peer award with a short note. Each employee can give four awards per quarter.

## Annual awards

Once a year a committee of employees chooses five people whose work had a large effect on customers.

## What awards include

Peer awards are a public thank-you without money. Annual award winners receive two extra days off.
