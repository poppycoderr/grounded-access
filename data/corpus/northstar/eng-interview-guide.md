# Engineering Interview Guide

This fictional guide is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## Skills interview

The skills interview uses a problem from the question bank that matches the level of the role. Interviewers do not invent new problems during the interview.

## Scoring

Each interviewer scores four areas on a scale from one to four before seeing anyone else's scores.

## Calibration

New interviewers shadow three interviews and are shadowed twice before they interview alone.
