# Bereavement Leave

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Paid leave

Employees receive five days of paid leave after the death of a partner, child or parent, and two days after the death of another relative.

## Taking the leave

The days do not have to be taken in one block and can be used within three months. Tell your manager; no certificate is needed.

## More time

Employees who need more time can take unpaid leave or arrange reduced hours with People Operations.
