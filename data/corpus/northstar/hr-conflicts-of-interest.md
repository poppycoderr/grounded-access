# Conflicts of Interest

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## What to declare

Employees declare any financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor, and any close relationship with someone they would manage or hire.

## How to declare

Declarations are made in the HR portal and reviewed by the legal team within ten working days.

## After a declaration

The legal team may ask that someone else takes a decision in which the employee has an interest. Declarations are kept confidential.
