# Jury Duty and Civic Duties

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Jury duty

Employees called for jury duty receive their full salary for the time they serve. Any allowance paid by the court for lost earnings is passed on to the company.

## Other civic duties

Up to five days per year are paid for other civic duties, such as working at a polling station or serving as a volunteer firefighter.

## Notifying the company

Tell your manager as soon as you receive the summons, and upload a copy to the HR portal.
