# Support Ticket Priorities

This fictional guide tells Northstar Cloud support agents which priority to give a customer ticket and how quickly each priority must be answered.

## Priorities

| Priority | When to use it | First response | Updates |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | The customer cannot use the product at all, or billing data is wrong for many invoices | 30 minutes, day and night | every hour |
| P2 | An important feature does not work and there is no workaround | 4 business hours | every business day |
| P3 | A feature does not work but a workaround exists | 1 business day | every three business days |
| P4 | A question, a feature request or a cosmetic problem | 3 business days | when something changes |

## Choosing a priority

Agents choose the priority from the impact on the customer, not from how upset the customer is or from the size of the account. When an agent cannot decide between two priorities, the higher one is chosen and a team lead reviews it within one business day.

## Raising and lowering

A customer may ask for a higher priority at any time. The agent raises it if the impact has grown. Only a team lead may lower the priority of a ticket that the customer opened as P1 or P2, and the agent must tell the customer why.

## Relation to outages

A P1 ticket is not the same as an engineering incident. When several P1 tickets describe the same symptom within 15 minutes, the agent who notices it opens an incident for the on-call engineer and links every ticket to it.
