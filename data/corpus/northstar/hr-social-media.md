# Social Media Guidelines

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Speaking for yourself

Employees may say where they work on social media. When they discuss the company, they make clear that they speak for themselves.

## Confidential information

Customer names, unreleased features and incidents that have not been announced on the status page are never discussed in public.

## Press questions

Questions from journalists are passed on to the communications team without comment.
