# Changing Your Name or Personal Details

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Where to change details

Employees change their legal name, address and bank details in the HR portal. A change of legal name needs a copy of an official document.

## Accounts

A new name is applied to the email address, chat and the directory within five working days. The old email address keeps forwarding mail for one year.

## Chosen names

Employees may use a chosen first name in all internal systems at any time, without any document.
