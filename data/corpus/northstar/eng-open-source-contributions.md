# Contributing to Open Source

This fictional policy is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## Contributions at work

Engineers may contribute fixes to open source projects that Northstar Cloud uses, during working time, without approval.

## New projects

Publishing a new open source project from company code needs approval from the head of engineering and the legal team.

## Contributor agreements

Contributor licence agreements are signed by the legal team on behalf of the company, never by individual engineers.
