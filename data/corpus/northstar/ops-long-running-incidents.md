# Long-running Incidents

This fictional guide describes how Northstar Cloud works.

## Shifts

An incident that lasts longer than four hours is staffed in shifts. Nobody works more than six hours in a row on an incident.

## Handover

Each shift change is written in the incident channel, with the current state, the open hypotheses and the next planned step.

## Customer updates

The communications lead keeps the status page updates going during the shift change.

## Afterwards

People who worked on an incident at night or for more than eight hours in one day start the next day late or take the day off, without asking for leave. The incident commander makes sure that this is respected, because tired engineers make the mistakes that turn one incident into the next one. Managers do not schedule demanding work for those people on the following day, and the follow-up actions of the incident are assigned only after everyone has rested. When an incident runs over several days, the head of engineering decides whether to bring in engineers from other teams, so that the people closest to the system are not the only ones carrying it.
