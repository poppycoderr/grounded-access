# Renewals

This fictional procedure is for the sales department only. It describes how account executives renew annual contracts.

## Timeline

The renewal conversation starts 120 days before the contract ends. A renewal quote is sent no later than 60 days before the end date.

## Automatic renewal

Contracts renew automatically for one year unless either side gives notice at least 30 days before the end date.

## At-risk renewals

A renewal is marked as at risk when the customer's usage has fallen by more than 30 percent in the last quarter, or when the customer has asked for a contract end date. At-risk renewals are reviewed every week by the sales director.
