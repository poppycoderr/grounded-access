# Device Encryption

This fictional standard says how Northstar Cloud protects the data on employee laptops and phones.

## Laptops

Every laptop has full-disk encryption turned on before it is handed to an employee. Device management checks every day that encryption is still on, and a laptop on which it has been turned off loses access to company email and chat within one hour.

## Phones

A phone that is used for work apps must have a screen lock with at least six digits or a biometric unlock. Work apps keep their data in an encrypted container that is wiped if the phone is removed from device management.

## Removable storage

Customer data may not be copied to USB drives or other removable storage. Exceptions are granted by the security team for a single transfer and require an encrypted drive provided by IT.
