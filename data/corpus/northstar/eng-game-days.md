# Game Days

This fictional guide is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## Purpose

A game day is a planned exercise in which a team causes a failure in a staging or production system to check that alerts, runbooks and people respond as expected.

## Frequency

Every team that owns a tier 1 service runs at least one game day per quarter.

## Safety

A game day in production has a named person who can stop the exercise at any moment and a rollback prepared in advance.
