# Code of Conduct

This fictional code describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Respect

Everyone at Northstar Cloud treats colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated, in the office, online or at company events.

## Raising concerns

Concerns can be raised with a manager, with People Operations or anonymously through the ethics line. Nobody who raises a concern in good faith is punished for it.

## Consequences

Breaches of the code can lead to disciplinary action up to dismissal. Every report is investigated by someone outside the reporting line of the people involved.
