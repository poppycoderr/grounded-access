# Monitors and Peripherals in the Office

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Office monitors

Every bookable desk has two monitors and a docking station.

## Keyboards and headsets

Keyboards, mice and headsets are available from the IT vending machine on each floor; the badge records who took what.

## Broken peripherals

Broken peripherals are returned to the vending machine's return slot and replaced at once.
