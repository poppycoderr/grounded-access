# Office Kitchens

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Shared food

Fruit, coffee and tea are free. Food in the shared fridge is labelled with a name and a date.

## Cleaning

The fridge is emptied every Friday at 15:00. Unlabelled food is thrown away.

## Allergies

Food at company events is labelled with the 14 major allergens.
