# Support Escalation Contacts

This fictional page is for the Northstar Cloud support department only.

## Enterprise escalations

For an Enterprise customer who cannot log in for more than 30 minutes, the agent calls the duty manager on the escalation line instead of opening a ticket.

## Goodwill credits

A support agent may grant a goodwill credit of up to 200 USD without approval. A team lead may grant up to 1,000 USD.

## Abusive contacts

An agent may end a call after one warning if the caller is abusive, and records the reason in the ticket.
