# Executive Escalations

This fictional guide is for the Northstar Cloud support department only.

## What it is

An executive escalation is a complaint that a customer sends to a Northstar Cloud executive instead of support.

## Handling

The executive's assistant forwards the message to the support team lead, who answers the customer within one business day.

## Priority

Executive escalations are not given a higher priority than the impact justifies; the priority rules apply as for any ticket.
