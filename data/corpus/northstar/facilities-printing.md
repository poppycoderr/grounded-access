# Printing

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Secure printing

Print jobs are released at the printer with the building badge. Jobs that are not released are deleted after 24 hours.

## Confidential paper

Confidential documents are put in the locked shredding bins, never in the normal paper recycling.

## Colour

Printing is black and white by default; colour printing can be selected per job.
