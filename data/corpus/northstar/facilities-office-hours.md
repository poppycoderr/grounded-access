# Office Opening Hours

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Opening hours

Offices are open from 07:00 to 21:00 on working days. Outside those hours the building badge opens the door only for people with an approved late access request.

## Weekends

Offices are closed at weekends. Weekend access is requested from the facilities team at least two working days in advance.

## Working alone

Employees working alone in the office after 21:00 register in the workplace app so that security knows they are in the building.
