# Learning and Development Budget

This fictional policy describes the money and time that Northstar Cloud gives each employee for learning.

## Annual budget

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,200 EUR per calendar year. The budget covers courses, books, certification exams and conference tickets. Travel to a conference is paid from the travel budget, not from the learning budget.

## Unused budget

Up to 400 EUR of an unused budget carries over to the next calendar year. Anything above that is lost on 31 December.

## Learning days

Every employee may spend three working days per year on learning without taking leave. Learning days must be agreed with the manager at least two weeks in advance.

## Certifications

The company pays for a failed certification exam once. A second failed attempt at the same exam is paid by the employee.

## Leaving the company

An employee who leaves within six months of a course or exam that cost more than 800 EUR repays half of its cost.
