# Email Guidelines

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## External email

Emails to customers come from the shared team addresses where one exists, so that answers do not depend on one person.

## Large attachments

Files larger than 20 MB are shared as links to the shared drive, not attached.

## Out of office

Set an out-of-office reply for any absence longer than one working day, naming a colleague to contact.
