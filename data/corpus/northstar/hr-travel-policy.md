# Business Travel and Expenses

This fictional policy describes how Northstar Cloud employees book travel and claim expenses.

## Booking

Book flights and hotels through the company travel tool. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

## Daily allowance

The meal allowance is 60 EUR per day for travel inside the EU and 75 USD per day for travel inside the US. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Submitting expenses

Submit receipts within 30 days of returning from the trip. Claims submitted later than 90 days are rejected.
