# Writing Knowledge Base Articles

This fictional guide is for the Northstar Cloud support department only. It explains when and how agents write articles for the public help center.

## When to write an article

An agent writes or updates an article when the same question has been answered in at least three tickets in one month. A ticket that was solved with an existing article is linked to that article.

## Review

Every new article is reviewed by a second agent and, if it describes product behaviour, by the product team that owns the feature. Articles are published only on weekdays so that someone can correct mistakes quickly.

## Keeping articles current

Every article has an owner. Articles that have not been reviewed for 12 months are hidden from the help center until their owner confirms them.
