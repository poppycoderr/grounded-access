# Leaving the Company

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Resigning

Employees resign in writing to their manager. People Operations confirms the last working day within three working days.

## Exit interview

Every leaver is offered an exit interview with People Operations. The notes are anonymized before they are shared with management.

## Last day

On the last day the employee returns their equipment and badge. Access to all systems ends at 18:00 local time.
