# Whistleblowing

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## What can be reported

Employees can report suspected fraud, bribery, breaches of law and serious risks to customers through the whistleblowing channel.

## The channel

The channel is run by an outside provider and can be used without giving a name. The provider passes reports to the chair of the audit committee, not to management.

## Feedback

The person who reported receives an acknowledgement within seven days and feedback on the outcome within three months.
