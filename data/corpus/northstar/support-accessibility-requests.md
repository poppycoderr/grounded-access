# Accessibility Requests from Customers

This fictional guide is for the Northstar Cloud support department only.

## Alternative formats

Customers can ask for invoices in large print or as accessible PDF documents. The request is passed to the billing team and handled within five business days.

## Assistive technology

Problems with screen readers are filed as accessibility bugs with the priority of a feature that does not work.

## Calls

Agents offer a video call with live captions to customers who ask for it.
