# Relocation Questions

This fictional page describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Temporary housing

Questions about temporary housing after a move go to the relocation partner named in the relocation letter.

## Language courses

Employees who move to a country whose language they do not speak may attend a language course during working hours for the first six months.

## Family members

Partners of relocated employees can use the relocation partner's job search support for one year.
