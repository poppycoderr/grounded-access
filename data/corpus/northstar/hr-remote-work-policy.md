# Remote Work Policy

This fictional policy explains how Northstar Cloud employees work away from the office.

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after their first 90 days. Roles that require on-site hardware access, such as data center technicians, are excluded.

## Home office stipend

Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 EUR in the EU or 550 USD in the US. The stipend covers a desk, a chair or a monitor and must be claimed within six months of becoming eligible.

## Core hours

Everyone is expected to be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone. Meetings that include more than one region should be scheduled inside the overlap of those core hours.

## Working from another country

Working from a country other than your employment country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and needs approval from People Operations because of tax and immigration rules.
