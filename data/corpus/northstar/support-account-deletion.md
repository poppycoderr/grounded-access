# Account Deletion Requests

This fictional guide is for the Northstar Cloud support department only.

## Who may ask

Only an account administrator can ask for the deletion of a whole account.

## Waiting period

Deleted accounts are kept in a disabled state for 30 days, during which the administrator can cancel the deletion.

## After deletion

After the 30 days the account data is deleted from production systems. The administrator receives a confirmation email.
