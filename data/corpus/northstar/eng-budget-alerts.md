# Cloud Budget Alerts

This fictional standard is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## Budgets

Each team has a monthly cloud budget agreed with finance at the start of the year.

## Alerts

The team lead is alerted when forecast spending for the month passes 80 percent and 100 percent of the budget.

## Overruns

A team that expects to exceed its budget by more than 10 percent explains the reason to the head of platform before the end of the month.
