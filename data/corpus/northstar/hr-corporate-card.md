# Corporate Card Policy

This fictional policy explains how Northstar Cloud employees use the company credit card. This version replaces the earlier policy; the limits and the receipt deadline have changed.

## Who receives a card

Employees who travel at least four times a year, and every team lead, receive a corporate card on request.

## Limits

A single transaction may not exceed 2,500 EUR. The monthly limit of a card is 6,000 EUR. A team lead may ask the finance team to raise the limit of a card for one month, for example before a conference.

## Receipts

Upload a receipt for every transaction to the expense tool within 10 days. A transaction that still has no receipt after 10 days blocks the card until the receipt is uploaded.

## Lost cards

Report a lost or stolen card to the card provider immediately and to the finance team on the same day.
