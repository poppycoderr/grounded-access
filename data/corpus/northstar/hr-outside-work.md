# Outside Work and Side Projects

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Side jobs

Employees may take a paid side job if it does not compete with Northstar Cloud and does not take more than ten hours a week. The manager must be told before the job starts.

## Open source

Contributions to open source projects in private time do not need approval, as long as they do not use company code or confidential information.

## Company equipment

Company laptops and accounts are not used for outside work.
