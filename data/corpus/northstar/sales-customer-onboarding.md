# Enterprise Customer Onboarding

This fictional guide describes the first 90 days of a new Enterprise customer at Northstar Cloud.

## Kickoff

A kickoff call takes place within five business days of the signed contract. The account executive, a solutions engineer and the customer's project lead agree on the goals for the first 90 days and on who is responsible for each one.

## Milestones

| Day | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 5 | kickoff call held |
| 30 | single sign-on configured and the first invoices imported |
| 60 | all planned users invited |
| 90 | onboarding review with the customer |

## Onboarding review

At the 90-day review the solutions engineer hands the account over to the customer success team. Goals that were not met are written into the handover with a new date and an owner.
