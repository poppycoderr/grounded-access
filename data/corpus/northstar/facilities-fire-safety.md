# Fire Safety

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## When the alarm sounds

Leave the building by the nearest marked exit and go to the assembly point. Do not use the lifts and do not collect your belongings.

## Fire wardens

Each floor has at least two trained fire wardens, who wear orange vests and check that their floor is empty.

## Drills

Each office holds a fire drill twice a year. The time is not announced in advance.
