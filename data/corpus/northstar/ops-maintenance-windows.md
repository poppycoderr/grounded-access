# Maintenance Windows

This fictional standard describes how Northstar Cloud works.

## Windows

Planned maintenance that may affect customers takes place on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 in the time zone of the affected region.

## Announcement

Maintenance is announced on the status page at least five business days in advance.

## Emergency maintenance

Emergency maintenance can happen at any time, and is announced as soon as it is decided.
