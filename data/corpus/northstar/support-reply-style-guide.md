# Support Reply Style Guide

This fictional guide is for the Northstar Cloud support department only. It explains how agents write replies to customers.

## Tone

Write as one person to another. Use the customer's name, say what you did and what happens next, and avoid internal names of teams, systems and tickets.

## Structure

Start with the answer or the next step, not with an apology. Keep a reply under 150 words unless the customer asked for detail. Put steps the customer has to take in a numbered list.

## Promises

Never promise a date for a fix that engineering has not confirmed in the incident or the bug ticket. Say instead when the customer will hear from support next, and keep that promise.

## Macros

Agents may start from a saved reply, called a macro, but must change at least the first sentence so that it answers the customer's actual question. A macro that has not been used for six months is archived by the support operations team.
