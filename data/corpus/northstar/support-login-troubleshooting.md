# Troubleshooting Customer Login Problems

This fictional support guide helps agents resolve login problems reported by customers.

## Locked accounts

An account locks after five failed login attempts and unlocks automatically after 15 minutes. Agents can unlock it sooner from the admin console after verifying the customer's identity.

## Single sign-on errors

If a customer sees "SAML response not valid", ask for the time on their identity provider server. A clock skew of more than three minutes causes this error.

## Escalation

Escalate to the identity engineering team if more than ten customers of the same tenant report login failures within one hour.
