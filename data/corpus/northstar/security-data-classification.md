# Data Classification Standard

This fictional standard defines how Northstar Cloud classifies information.

## Levels

- Public: approved for anyone, such as marketing pages.
- Internal: available to all employees, such as most policies.
- Confidential: limited to teams with a business need, such as customer contracts and incident details.
- Restricted: limited to named individuals, such as encryption keys and payroll data.

## Handling rules

Confidential and restricted information must never be pasted into external tools. Restricted information must be stored only in the secrets manager or the HR system.
