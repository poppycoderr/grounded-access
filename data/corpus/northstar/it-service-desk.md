# IT Service Desk

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Reaching the desk

The IT service desk is reached through the IT portal or the #it-help channel from 08:00 to 19:00 on working days.

## Urgent problems

Outside those hours, urgent problems that stop someone from working are reported by phone to the on-call IT engineer.

## Response targets

Requests are answered within four working hours; a request that blocks a whole team is answered within one hour.
