# Phone Support Hours

This fictional page describes how Northstar Cloud works.

## Hours

Phone support is available on working days from 08:00 to 20:00 in the EU and from 08:00 to 18:00 Eastern Time in the US.

## Who can call

Phone support is included in the Enterprise plan. Customers on the Standard plan use email and chat.

## Outside hours

Outside phone hours, Enterprise customers with a P1 problem call the emergency line, which forwards to the on-duty agent.
