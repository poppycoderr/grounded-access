# Status Page Guidelines

This fictional guide explains how Northstar Cloud writes updates on its public status page. It is public so that customers can see what they may expect.

## When an incident is posted

An incident is posted on the status page within 15 minutes of being confirmed if it affects customers in any region. Planned maintenance is announced at least five business days in advance.

## What an update says

Each update says which services and regions are affected, what customers can observe, and when the next update will be posted. Updates do not speculate about causes before the cause is known.

## How often

During an incident an update is posted at least every 30 minutes, even if nothing has changed. The final update says when the incident ended, and a summary follows within five business days for incidents that lasted longer than one hour.
