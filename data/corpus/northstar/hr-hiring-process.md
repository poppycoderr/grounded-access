# Hiring Process

This fictional guide describes how Northstar Cloud hires employees, from an open role to a signed offer.

## Opening a role

A manager opens a role in the hiring tool with a description, the level and the location. The role is published only after the department head has approved it.

## Interview stages

| Stage | Who runs it | Length |
|---|---|---|
| screening call | a recruiter | 30 minutes |
| skills interview | two people from the team | 60 minutes |
| work sample | the candidate, at home | at most 3 hours of work |
| final conversation | the hiring manager and one person from another team | 45 minutes |

A candidate is never asked to spend more than three hours on a work sample, and the work sample is never used in production.

## Decisions

Every interviewer writes their feedback before talking to the other interviewers, so that one opinion does not shape the others. Feedback must be written within one business day of the interview. The hiring manager makes the decision after reading all feedback; a candidate who receives a strong no from any interviewer is discussed in a meeting before an offer is made.

## Referrals

An employee who refers a candidate who is hired receives a referral bonus of 1,500 EUR after the new employee has completed six months. Managers do not receive a bonus for hires into their own team.

## Candidate data

Applications of candidates who were not hired are deleted 12 months after the last contact, unless the candidate agreed to be considered for later roles.
