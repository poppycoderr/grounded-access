# Workplace Adjustments

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Asking for an adjustment

Employees who need an adjustment because of a disability or a health condition ask People Operations. No diagnosis has to be shared.

## Examples

Adjustments include special equipment, changed working hours, a quiet workspace or more time for written tasks.

## Timeline

People Operations answers within ten working days, and the cost of adjustments is never charged to the team's budget.
