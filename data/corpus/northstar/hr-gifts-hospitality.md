# Gifts and Hospitality

This fictional policy describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Accepting gifts

Employees may accept gifts from customers or suppliers worth up to 50 EUR. Anything of higher value is declined politely or handed to the legal team.

## Hospitality

Business meals with customers or suppliers are acceptable if they are reasonable and not offered during a running tender.

## Register

Every gift above 20 EUR is recorded in the gifts register, whether it was accepted or declined.
