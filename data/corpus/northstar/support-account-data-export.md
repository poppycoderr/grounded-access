# Customer Account Data Exports

This fictional procedure is for the Northstar Cloud support department only. It explains how an agent handles a customer's request for a full export of the data held in their account.

## Who may ask

Only an account administrator may ask for a full export. The agent checks the requester against the administrator list in the account console; a request from anyone else is declined and the administrators are told that it was made.

## Verifying the request

Before starting an export the agent calls the administrator back on the phone number stored in the account, never on a number given in the ticket. An export is never started on the strength of an email alone.

## Producing the export

The agent starts the export from the internal admin console. The export is encrypted, and the download link is sent to the administrator's stored email address. The link expires after 72 hours and can be used three times.

## Deadlines

The export must be delivered within 10 calendar days of the verified request. If the account holds more than 500 GB, the agent asks a team lead to plan the export with the data platform team, and the customer is told the new date within those 10 days.
