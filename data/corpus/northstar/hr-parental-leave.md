# Parental Leave

This fictional policy describes leave for new parents at Northstar Cloud.

## Primary caregivers

The primary caregiver receives 18 weeks of fully paid leave, which must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Secondary caregivers

The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be split into at most two blocks.

## Returning to work

Parents may return on a reduced schedule of 60 percent of their usual hours for the first four weeks without any change to their salary.
