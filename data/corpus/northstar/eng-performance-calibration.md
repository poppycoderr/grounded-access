# Engineering Performance Calibration

This fictional procedure is confidential and is for engineering only. It describes how engineering managers calibrate performance ratings before they are shared with employees.

## Ratings

Every engineer receives one of four ratings: below expectations, meets expectations, exceeds expectations, or outstanding. There is no quota for any rating.

## Calibration meetings

Managers of the same department meet twice a year, in March and in September, to compare their proposed ratings. Each manager presents the evidence for every rating above or below meets expectations. A rating changes only if the group agrees that the evidence does not support it.

## After calibration

Ratings are shared with each engineer in a one-to-one conversation within two weeks of the calibration meeting. Engineers rated below expectations receive a written development plan in the same conversation, with goals for the next three months.

## Confidentiality

Calibration notes are kept in the confidential HR folder and deleted after three years. Managers do not discuss the ratings of one engineer with another.
