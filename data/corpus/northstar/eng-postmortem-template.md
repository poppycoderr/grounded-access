# Postmortem Template

This fictional guide is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## Sections

A postmortem has a summary, a timeline, the root cause, what went well and the follow-up actions.

## Blameless

A postmortem names systems and decisions, not people. It asks why a decision made sense at the time it was made.

## Follow-up actions

Each follow-up action has an owner and a due date, and open actions are reviewed in the weekly operations meeting.
