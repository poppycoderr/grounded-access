# Partner Program

This fictional guide is for the sales and support departments. It describes how Northstar Cloud works with resellers and consulting partners.

## Partner levels

Registered partners have signed the partner agreement. Certified partners also employ at least two people who passed the Northstar Cloud administrator exam. Only certified partners may resell the Enterprise plan.

## Deal registration

A partner registers a deal in the partner portal before the first meeting with the customer. A registered deal is protected for 90 days: no other partner can register the same customer during that time.

## Support for partner customers

Customers who bought through a partner open tickets with the partner first. The partner escalates to Northstar Cloud support with the partner's own support identifier, and the agent answers the partner, not the customer, unless the partner asks otherwise.

## Leaving the program

A partner that has not registered a deal for 18 months is moved back from certified to registered.
