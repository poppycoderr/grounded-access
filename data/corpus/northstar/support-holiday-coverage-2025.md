# Support Coverage over the 2025 Year-End Period

This fictional plan applied only from 20 December 2025 to 4 January 2026. It describes how Northstar Cloud support was staffed while many agents were on leave.

## Staffing

Each region keeps at least two agents on duty per shift. Agents who work on 24, 25 or 31 December or on 1 January receive one extra day off for each of those days.

## Response targets

P1 tickets keep their normal first response target. For P2 tickets the first response target is extended to 8 business hours, and P3 and P4 tickets are answered from 5 January 2026.
