# Bicycle Parking

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Where to park

Bicycles are parked in the basement bike room, which opens with the building badge. Bicycles may not be brought into the offices.

## Showers

Showers and changing rooms are next to the bike room. Towels are provided.

## Long-term storage

Bicycles left in the bike room for more than four weeks without being moved are labelled and removed after a further two weeks.
