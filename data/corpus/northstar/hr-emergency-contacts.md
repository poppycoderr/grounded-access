# Emergency Contacts

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Why we ask

People Operations keeps one or two emergency contacts for each employee, to be called only if the employee is injured or missing at work or on a business trip.

## Keeping them current

Employees review their emergency contacts every year when the HR portal asks them to.

## Who can see them

Emergency contacts are visible only to People Operations and to the security team during an emergency.
