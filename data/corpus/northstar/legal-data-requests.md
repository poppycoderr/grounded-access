# Requests for Customer Data from Authorities

This fictional policy describes how Northstar Cloud works.

## Handling

Every request from a public authority for customer data is passed to the legal team on the day it arrives. No employee answers it directly.

## Review

The legal team checks that the request is lawful and as narrow as possible, and challenges requests that are not.

## Telling the customer

The customer is told about the request unless the law forbids it.
