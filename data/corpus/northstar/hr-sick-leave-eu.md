# Sick Leave: European Union Offices

This fictional policy applies to Northstar Cloud employees employed in the EU.

## Reporting sickness

Tell your manager before your normal start time on the first day of sickness. You do not need to say what is wrong.

## Medical certificate

A medical certificate is needed from the fourth calendar day of sickness.

## Pay

Employees receive their full salary for the first six weeks of sickness in a calendar year. After that, statutory sick pay applies.
