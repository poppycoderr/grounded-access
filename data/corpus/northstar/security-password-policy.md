# Password and Sign-in Standard

This fictional standard describes how Northstar Cloud employees sign in to company systems.

## Passwords

A password has at least 14 characters. Passwords are not changed on a schedule; they are changed when there is any sign that they may be known to someone else. A password used for a Northstar Cloud account must not be used for any other account.

## Multi-factor authentication

Every employee account requires multi-factor authentication with a hardware security key or the company authenticator app. Codes sent by text message are not accepted.

## Lockout

An account is locked for 15 minutes after ten failed sign-in attempts within an hour. The IT service desk can unlock it earlier after verifying the employee by video call.

## Password manager

Every employee receives a licence for the company password manager and is expected to store all work passwords in it.
