# Engineering Onboarding Plan

This fictional guide is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## First week

New engineers set up their laptop, deploy a small change to staging and meet the people they will work with most.

## First month

By the end of the first month a new engineer has shipped a change to production and joined the on-call rotation as a shadow.

## Buddy

Every new engineer has a buddy from their team for the first three months.
