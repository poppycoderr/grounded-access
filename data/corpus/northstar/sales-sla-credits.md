# Service Level Agreement Credits

This fictional document explains the service credits in Northstar Cloud Enterprise contracts.

## Credit schedule

If monthly uptime falls below 99.95 percent, the customer receives a credit of 10 percent of that month's fee. Below 99.5 percent the credit is 25 percent, and below 99 percent it is 50 percent.

## Claiming a credit

Customers must claim a credit within 30 days after the end of the affected month by opening a support ticket.

## Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime.
