# Mobile App Releases

This fictional procedure is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## Release train

The mobile apps are released every two weeks on Tuesday. A change that misses the release waits for the next one.

## Staged rollout

Each release goes to 10 percent of users first and to everyone after three days without a rise in crashes.

## Hotfixes

A hotfix outside the train needs approval from the mobile lead and is only made for crashes or security problems.
