# Work Equipment

This fictional policy covers the laptops and phones that Northstar Cloud provides to its employees.

## Laptops

Every employee receives a laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every three years, or earlier if a repair would cost more than half the price of a new one. Engineers may choose between two models; everyone else receives the standard model.

## Phones

Employees who are on call, and every manager, receive a company phone. Others may use their own phone for work apps after enrolling it in device management.

## Returning equipment

Equipment is returned on the last working day. An employee who leaves may buy their laptop for 10 percent of its original price if it is older than two years and contains no customer data.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within 24 hours. The device is wiped remotely as soon as it is reported.
