# Office Visitors

This fictional policy explains how visitors are received in Northstar Cloud offices. It is public so that visitors and contractors can read it before they arrive.

## Before the visit

The host registers every visitor in the visitor system at least one working day in advance. Walk-in visitors are only admitted if their host comes to the reception.

## At the reception

Visitors show an identity document and receive a badge that is valid for one day. The badge must be worn visibly and returned when leaving.

## In the office

Visitors are accompanied by their host at all times outside meeting rooms. Visitors may not enter the network rooms, even with their host.
