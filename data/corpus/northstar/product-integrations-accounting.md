# Accounting Integrations

This fictional page is part of the public Northstar Cloud product documentation.

## Sync

Invoices and payments are synced to connected accounting systems every 15 minutes.

## Conflicts

If a record was changed in both systems, the change in Northstar Cloud wins and the conflict is listed in the integration log.

## Disconnecting

Disconnecting an integration stops the sync; records already synced stay in the accounting system.
