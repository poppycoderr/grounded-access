# Moving to Another Team

This fictional policy explains how Northstar Cloud employees move to another team or role inside the company.

## Who can apply

Employees can apply for an internal role after 12 months in their current role. An employee who is on a written development plan cannot apply until the plan has ended.

## Telling your manager

Tell your current manager before your first interview for the internal role. Managers may not block an application, but they may agree a later start date of up to two months so that the team can plan the handover.

## Interviews

Internal candidates go through the same interview stages as external candidates, except for the screening call. The hiring manager gives every internal candidate who is not selected a conversation about the reasons.

## Pay

Moving to a role at the same level does not change pay. A move to a higher level follows the normal promotion rules.
