# Sick Leave: United States Offices

This fictional policy applies to Northstar Cloud employees employed in the US.

## Reporting sickness

Tell your manager before your normal start time on the first day of sickness. You do not need to say what is wrong.

## Paid sick days

Employees receive ten paid sick days per calendar year. Unused sick days do not carry over.

## Medical note

A note from a doctor is needed only after three consecutive sick days.
