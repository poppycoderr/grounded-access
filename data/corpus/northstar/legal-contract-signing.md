# Signing Contracts

This fictional policy describes how Northstar Cloud works.

## Authority

Customer contracts up to an annual value of 100,000 EUR can be signed by the sales director. Larger contracts are signed by the chief revenue officer.

## Supplier contracts

Supplier contracts are signed by the budget owner if the value is within their budget and the legal team has approved the terms.

## Electronic signatures

Contracts are signed electronically through the contract system.
