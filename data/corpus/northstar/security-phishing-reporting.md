# Reporting Phishing

This fictional guide tells Northstar Cloud employees what to do with a suspicious email, message or call.

## Recognizing phishing

Be suspicious of any message that asks you to sign in through a link, to pay or change bank details, or to share a code from your authenticator app. Northstar Cloud IT never asks for a code by email, chat or phone.

## Reporting

Use the report button in the mail client. It sends the message to the security team and removes it from your inbox. If the message arrived in chat or by phone, post a short description in the #security-report channel.

## If you clicked

If you clicked a link or entered your password, report it at once, change your password and tell the IT service desk, even outside working hours. Nobody is blamed for clicking; the sooner it is reported, the less damage is done.

## Phishing exercises

The security team sends simulated phishing emails four times a year. Employees who report a simulated email are counted as having passed. Results are reported per department, never per person.
