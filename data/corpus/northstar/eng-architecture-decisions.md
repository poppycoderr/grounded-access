# Architecture Decision Records

This fictional guide is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## When to write one

An architecture decision record is written for any decision that would be expensive to reverse, such as a new datastore, a new language or a change to service boundaries.

## Format

A record states the context, the options considered, the decision and its consequences, on one page.

## Review

Records are reviewed in the weekly architecture meeting and stored in the repository of the affected service.
