# Purchase Orders

This fictional procedure is for the Northstar Cloud finance department only.

## When a purchase order is needed

Every purchase above 5,000 EUR needs a purchase order before the supplier is engaged.

## Approval

Purchase orders are approved by the budget owner and, above 50,000 EUR, by the chief financial officer.

## Invoices without an order

Supplier invoices that do not match a purchase order are returned to the supplier.
