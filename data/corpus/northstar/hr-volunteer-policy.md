# Volunteer Time Off Policy

Northstar Cloud encourages employees to support charitable and community organizations. This policy is fictional and exists only for the Grounded Access demo corpus.

## Eligibility

All permanent employees who have completed their probation period are eligible. Contractors are not covered by this policy.

## European Union

Employees based in the EU receive two paid volunteer days per calendar year. Unused volunteer days do not carry over into the next year.

## United States

Employees based in the US receive one paid volunteer day per calendar year. Managers may approve one additional unpaid day for company-organized events.

## How to request

Submit the request in the HR portal at least five working days in advance and name the organization you will support.
