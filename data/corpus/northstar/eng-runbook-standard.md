# Runbook Standard

This fictional standard is part of the Northstar Cloud engineering handbook.

## Structure

Every runbook has the same sections: when to use it, checks before acting, the steps, and how to confirm the result.

## Ownership

Runbooks live in the service ownership register's runbook folder and are owned by the service's team.

## Testing

Runbooks for tier 1 services are followed step by step during a game day at least once a year.
