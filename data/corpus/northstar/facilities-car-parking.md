# Car Parking

This fictional guide describes how things work at Northstar Cloud.

## Spaces

The offices have a small number of parking spaces. Spaces for people with disabilities and for parents with young children are reserved first.

## Booking

Remaining spaces are booked in the workplace app on the day, first come first served.

## Charging

Electric cars can be charged at the marked spaces; charging is free, and cars are moved once they are charged.
