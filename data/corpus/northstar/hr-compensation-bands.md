# Compensation Bands

This fictional document lists salary bands for Northstar Cloud engineering roles. It is confidential.

## Salary bands

A senior engineer in Germany is paid between 92,000 and 118,000 EUR per year. A staff engineer in Germany is paid between 121,000 and 149,000 EUR per year.

## On-call pay

Engineers at staff level and above receive an on-call allowance of 400 EUR per week of primary on-call instead of the standard allowance.

## Annual review

Salary bands are reviewed every January. Managers receive the new bands two weeks before they tell their teams.
