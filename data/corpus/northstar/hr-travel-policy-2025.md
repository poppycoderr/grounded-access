# Business Travel and Expenses (2025)

This fictional policy was in force during 2025. It has been replaced by the current travel policy.

## Booking

Book flights and hotels through the company travel tool. Economy class is the default for all flights.

## Daily allowance

During 2025 the meal allowance was 50 EUR per day for travel inside the EU and 65 USD per day for travel inside the US.

## Submitting expenses

Submit receipts within 45 days of returning from the trip.
