# Corporate Card Policy

This fictional policy explains how Northstar Cloud employees use the company credit card.

## Who receives a card

Employees who travel at least four times a year, and every team lead, receive a corporate card on request.

## Limits

A single transaction may not exceed 1,500 EUR. The monthly limit of a card is 5,000 EUR.

## Receipts

Upload a receipt for every transaction to the expense tool within 30 days. A transaction without a receipt after 30 days is deducted from the next salary payment.

## Lost cards

Report a lost or stolen card to the card provider immediately and to the finance team on the same day.
