# Single Sign-on

This fictional page is part of the public Northstar Cloud product documentation.

## Availability

Single sign-on with SAML or OpenID Connect is available on the Enterprise plan.

## Enforcing

When single sign-on is enforced, users of the account can no longer sign in with a password; the owner keeps a password as a fallback.

## Provisioning

Users can be created and removed automatically through SCIM.
